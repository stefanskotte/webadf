The Old School Emulation Center
13/03/2025

Hello everyone - it's been a while, nearly a year between releases! We hope you'll agree that the wait was worth it as we unveil yet another bumper TOSEC release.

We'll start, as always, with the amazing work of our Commodore 64 and Amiga teams. These systems are so popular and huge that they represent a huge portion of the TOSEC catalogue all by themselves, and we're always amazed at the amount of work that duncantwain, Crashdisk and others put into not only adding the constant stream of new releases, but fixing and properly cataloguing literally millions of files across hundreds of DATs.

mictlantecuhtle continues in his quest to put his fingerprints on every single DAT in the set, with contributions this time spanning across nearly 600 DATs. This release again sees the addition of some wonderful and obscure machines, covering all shapes and sizes, from the handheld Casio PB-100 to the room-sized BCC 500 (which, while hosted at the University of Hawaii, acted as the hub of the ALOHA system, a precursor to the modern Ethernet). As well as many additions, this release also sees the continuation of efforts to address some of TOSEC's legacy DATs, most notably with the behemoth Tandy Radio Shack Model III and 4 "Various" DATs now split out into their separate types and extensions.

As always, thanks to those in our forums and Discord who have made their own contributions - a particular shoutout here to smesgr for significant contributions to the Atari 2600, GBA, PC and PSX DATs, but also in no particular order to eientei, Pugsy, UnlockerPT, Wiggy2k, Cactus, star, whatever000 and artifexor, all of whom have made contributions big or small towards this release.

The ADF scanners have laid dormant in the TOSEC-PIX offices once again, with members concentrating on adding more of the backlog. That being said, the TOSEC-PIX update is the largest ever, weighing in at close to 750GB with 242 new and 20 updated dats!

At the TOSEC-ISO offices, things are still moving at a frenetic pace, with PlayStation 2 WIP dats from 2009 added and also hundreds of submissions added from members worldwide. More donations of physical media have also been received from members to add to the project. Maddog has been busy as usual with his .gdi Dreamcast contributions, but with the excellent help from a new contributor, Captain Blood, they also introduced a couple of brand new shiny .cdi dats. Since .cdi is the only way to play using the original drive of the console, we felt that they were long overdue to be added in the quest to preserve everything about Dreamcast. Finally, this time we have some GameCube updates as well. Looks like only the original XBox was left out of this console generation, sorry big black box! We hope to correct this in the future.

A huge thanks to everyone who has donated either physical media or submitted disc images to the project, it is most appreciated.

Be sure to join our Discord channel if you haven’t already.

Enjoy this release and see you all in a few months!
